# Methylene blue

Synthetic dye first created in 1876 — precursor molecule to hydroxychloroquine/chloroquine; still used IV in hospitals to counteract mitochondrial metabolic poisons such as carbon monoxide and cyanide.

## Mechanisms

- Accepts electrons and transfers them directly to oxygen, creating a shortcut in the electron transport chain that boosts ATP production, oxygen use, and blood flow even in healthy individuals.
- Activates SIRT1 (a "longevity gene") and AMPK, enhancing fat burning (CPT-1, PPARα).
- Acts as a substitute electron acceptor in low-oxygen conditions, helping the liver break down estrogen; works synergistically with niacinamide (preserves NAD+).
- Blocks multiple inflammasomes (NLRP3, NLRC4, AIM2) and reduces pro-inflammatory IL-1β and IL-18.
- Hormetic: low doses are antioxidant, high doses are pro-oxidative.

## Benefits

- **Cognitive support** — influences dopamine and serotonin systems; shown to improve bipolar disorder and severe depression. Its stabilized form LMTM (hydromethylthionine) slows cognitive decline and brain atrophy in Alzheimer's by inhibiting tau protein aggregation.
- **Liver health** — reduces fat accumulation and inflammation in fatty liver disease, even in animals on a high-fat diet.
- **Anti-inflammatory** — improved survival in sepsis (LPS) and Listeria infection models.
- **Other** — helps protect against EMF/radiation exposure; promotes youthful skin (reduces ROS, stimulates cell proliferation); supports bone health against skeletal aging; mitigates reperfusion injury after a heart attack if given within minutes.

## Side effects and cautions

- Blue discoloration of urine/tongue at doses above 30–50 mg
- Pulse oximeter interference
- Serotonin syndrome risk when combined with SSRIs (doses >100 mg)
- Caution with severe renal insufficiency
- Contraindicated in G6PD deficiency (risk of hemolytic anemia)
- Possible GI discomfort, headaches, increased blood pressure
- Interactions with antidepressants and antimalarials

## How to use

- **Dose:** 3–5 mg once daily (half-life 12–13 h), with one day off per week for long-term use.
- **Higher doses** (>3–5 mg) only for life-threatening conditions (CO/cyanide poisoning, resistant UTI).
- **Form:** pharmaceutical-grade only, solid capsule/tablet via a compounding pharmacy. Avoid liquid solutions (degrade within 48–72 h via photodegradation, redox reactions, and contamination) and non-pharmaceutical products (heavy metal contamination risk).
