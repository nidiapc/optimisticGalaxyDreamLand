# Fruits (horticulture)

## How to germinate lemon seeds

1. Retrieve the seeds
2. Clean the seeds thoroughly
3. Remove the outer film (membrane)
4. Place on damp cotton
5. Wait for germination (approx. 7 days)
6. Plant the germinated seed
7. Growth of the first true leaves
8. Growing lemon plant
